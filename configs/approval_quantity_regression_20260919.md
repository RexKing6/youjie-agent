Development regression, not held-out effectiveness evidence. No ERP/MES writes.
Use existing authorized live model, no retries or endpoint changes. Record every result.

2. Full chain with paraphrased approval quantity; one run.
3. Negative full chain: same reply without documents; must not solve or release A2.
