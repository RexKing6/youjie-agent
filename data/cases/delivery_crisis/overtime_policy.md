# Overtime policy

Friday overtime may produce at most 20 Industrial Sensor Nodes, represented as two 10-unit lots.
Each overtime lot adds 400 CNY, or 40 CNY per unit. Overtime requires explicit planner approval and may
not be used for a plan whose recovery budget is already exhausted.
